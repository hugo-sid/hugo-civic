---
title: "Records request processing times improve"
date: 2026-06-22
author: "Office of Performance"
tags: ["Performance", "Public notice"]
description: "Median processing time for records requests fell to 18 days."
---

The median processing time for a records request fell to 18 days this quarter,
down from 26 days a year ago.

The backlog of requests older than a year is now in single figures.
